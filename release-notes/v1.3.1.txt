Uni-Vishwas v1.3.1 (versionCode 8)
===================================

Release date: 18 August 2026
Package:      com.univishwas.app
Track:        Internal testing -> Production
Targets:      Android 15 (API 35), minimum Android 5.0 (API 21)


What's new (Play Console "Release notes" field, <=500 chars)
------------------------------------------------------------
<en-US>
Big portal update! Log in simply with your house number and password. New in this release: society attendance tracking, parking and vehicle management with camera-based scanning, and AOA change records. Plus all the essentials - image uploads, poll PDF downloads, pull-to-refresh, dark mode and offline retry - working smoothly in one app.
</en-US>


Highlights
----------
- House number + password login: no OTP wait, sign in with the
  credentials issued for your flat (see Settings > Password
  Management on the portal).
- Attendance module: mark and review society attendance, with
  reports.
- Parking & vehicles: register vehicles to your flat, and scan
  vehicles in and out with the camera from inside the app.
- AOA changes: browse Articles of Association change records.
- Family members: manage family member accounts linked to your house.
- Existing features carried forward: notices, complaints with photo
  attachments, polls with PDF result downloads, classified ads,
  vendors, SPOCs, calendar, finance, profile with photo upload.

App-side conveniences (unchanged)
---------------------------------
- Stay signed in between sessions.
- Upload photos from gallery or camera.
- Poll PDFs download through Android's native download manager.
- Pull-to-refresh, hardware Back navigation, dark mode, offline
  retry screen.

Fixed
-----
- Resolved the "Version code 7 has already been used" Play Console
  upload error (versionCode bumped to 8). No functional changes
  compared to the v1.3.0 build.

For the reviewer
----------------
- The app is a WebView client for the society portal at
  learninganddevelopment.net/Uninav/Application.
- Test credentials: house number + password shared out-of-band via
  the App access declaration.
- Camera permission is used only for photo uploads and the vehicle
  scanning feature, both user-initiated.
