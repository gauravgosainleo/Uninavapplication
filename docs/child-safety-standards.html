Uni-Vishwas - Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 18 August 2026

These standards describe how the Uni-Vishwas app and the Uni-Vishwas residential-society portal it connects to (package name com.univishwas.app, operated by the Uninav Heights society management, "we", "us") prevent, detect and respond to child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). They are published in accordance with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy.

1. Zero tolerance for CSAE and CSAM

We prohibit any content or behaviour that sexualises, abuses, exploits or endangers children. This includes creating, uploading, sharing, requesting or linking to CSAM, child grooming, sextortion, the trafficking of minors, or any sexualised communication directed at or about a minor. This prohibition applies to every part of the service: profile photos, complaint attachments, notices, classified ads, vehicle records, polls and any other content residents can submit.

2. Who can use the service

Uni-Vishwas is a private community tool intended for adult residents of the Uninav Heights society. Accounts are tied to a verified house number issued by the society, and family-member accounts are created under the responsibility of the adult resident of that household. The app is not directed at children and provides no public social discovery, no direct messaging with strangers, and no anonymous participation.

3. In-app reporting

Any resident can report content or behaviour that concerns them directly from within the app, using the Complaints module, and the report is visible to society administrators for action. Reports can also be sent by email to the child-safety contact below. We aim to acknowledge reports within 24 hours.

4. How we respond

  • Reported content is reviewed promptly by society administrators.
  • Confirmed CSAE/CSAM content is removed immediately and the submitting account is suspended.
  • We report apparent CSAM to the relevant authorities, including the police and India's National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in), and cooperate fully with law-enforcement investigations, preserving evidence as the law requires.
  • We take action against accounts that facilitate or attempt to facilitate CSAE, up to permanent removal from the service.

5. Compliance with child safety laws

We comply with applicable child safety and child protection laws, including the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012 and the Information Technology Act, 2000 (India), and with our obligations to report CSAM to the authorities listed above.
